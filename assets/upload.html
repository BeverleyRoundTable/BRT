How to Link an Event Photo to beverleyroundtable.co.uk
1Post on Spond (as pending)
Post your event on Spond early as pending (leave everyone uninvited until details are locked in, then invite the group). Add Beverley Round Table as co-host so it syncs here (request Helper access if needed). No need to paste image URLs in Spond!
2Find your event below
Open the Upcoming Events tab (use the search box if there are lots of events).
3Add your photo
Drag & drop an image straight onto the event card, or click Upload Photo (or From Library to reuse an existing upload). Large photos are auto-optimised.
4Live immediately!
Your photo appears on beverleyroundtable.co.uk right away, replacing the AI fallback. No waiting for a sync.
Auto-cleanup: Event photos are automatically deleted from storage 14 days after the event finishes (recurring events & standalone Library uploads are kept). Need a link for Spond / WhatsApp / socials? Switch to the Upload & Library tab to upload any image and click Copy URL.
