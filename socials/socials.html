The Wednesday Formula

Simple, fair, and zero guesswork for current members & prospective guests. Every member organises two nights across the year (excluding December for the Santa Sleigh).

Start of Month · Paid

Paid Commercial Social

Book an activity, organised tour, or commercially run night out — axe throwing, karting, padel, clay shooting, brewery/venue tours, escape rooms, or curry & darts.

End of Month · Free

Creative Free / Sub-£20

Banter & brains over budget. Free to attend — including free organised tours (behind-the-scenes local visits, landmarks, stations, heritage walks) or up to £20 total for the group on props/accessories: Taskmaster nights, Westwood challenges, blind taste tests, or pub knockouts.

1 + 1 Quota · No Dec

One Paid + One Free Each

Each member claims 1 Paid & 1 Free social to lock in the 12-month calendar (5-week months with a 3rd alternate Wednesday include an Extra Social to keep the fortnightly schedule). December is excluded — all hands on deck for our Beverley Santa Sleigh tour!

Table Charter & Zero-Gap Planning

What's Expected of Every Tabler

Every member — including Programme Officers — pulls their weight by owning two nights across the year, while Programme Officers also coordinate the calendar so everything runs to plan.

5-Step Member Charter

Your responsibilities for your 1 Paid + 1 Free Wednesday

Every Tabler
  1. 1

    Claim Your 1 + 1 Early

    Put your name against 1 Paid Commercial Social (start of month) and 1 Creative Free / Sub-£20 Social (end of month) early in the year so we keep all 11 non-December months full.

  2. 2

    Take the Lead & Lock Logistics 3–4 Weeks Out

    You lead your night — book the venue/activity (start of month) or prep your sub-£20 props & rules (end of month), then switch your slot from Idea Claimed to Confirmed with the venue & plan ready to post on Spond.

  3. 3

    Post on Spond & Rally on WhatsApp (2 Weeks Out)

    Lock in the exact meeting time & RSVPs on Spond and hit WhatsApp Share on your calendar card two weeks out to rally the lads.

  4. 4

    Look After Prospective New Guys

    This calendar helps men thinking of joining see what we're about. Meet guests on arrival, introduce them round, and mix teams so newcomers feel part of the lads straight away.

  5. 5

    Zero Dark Wednesdays (Swaps & Cover)

    If work or family gets in the way, arrange a direct date swap with another Tabler or bring in a Co-Organiser buddy and update the planner — never leave a Wednesday blank.

Programme Officers

Calendar Coordinators · Keep Everything Running to Plan

Role of the Programme Officers: Programme Officers pitch in with their own 1 Paid + 1 Free social just like every other Tabler, and also help coordinate the calendar so everything runs to plan — nudging unconfirmed IDEA drafts, helping pair up co-organisers if needed, and making sure no Wednesday is left blank.

Google Sheet & Script Blueprint

How the 3-tab spreadsheet database and auto-sync script connect together.

1. Quick Deployment (2 Minutes)

  1. Create a blank Google Sheet (e.g. BRT Annual Socials Database) and keep Sheet sharing set to Restricted / Private to you.
  2. Open Extensions → Apps Script and paste socials/Code.gs from your GitHub repo.
  3. Run setupSocialsDatabase() once (or reload the Sheet and click 🍻 BRT Socials Admin → 1. Initialise) — it builds all 3 tabs and 12 months of alternate Wednesdays automatically (excluding December).
  4. Type your member names into Members Column B.
  5. Keep secrets out of Sheet cells: click 🍻 BRT Socials Admin → 5. Set Gemini API Key and 6. Set Table PIN / Admin PIN (stored privately in Script Properties).
  6. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone), then copy the /exec URL. Visitors view the calendar read-only, while saving socials or using AI requires the Table PIN.
Tab 1: Members
  • A: MemberID (auto)
  • B: Name (edit here!)
  • C: Role
  • D: PaidCount (auto)
  • E: FreeCount (auto)
  • F: PaidSocialTitle
  • G: FreeSocialTitle
  • H: QuotaStatus
  • I: Active (TRUE/FALSE)
  • J: ProgrammeOfficer (TRUE/FALSE)
Tab 2: Socials
  • A: SlotID (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • B-E: Date / Ordinal
  • F: SocialType (PAID/FREE)
  • H: Status (OPEN/IDEA/CONFIRMED)
  • I-J: Organizer / CoOrg
  • K-M: Title, Desc, Location
  • N-P: Start, End, Cost
  • Q-T: Accessories, Prospective, Img, Updated
Settings & Script Properties
  • 🔐 Script Prop: GEMINI_API_KEY
  • 🔒 Script Prop: TABLE_PIN (Private)
  • 🛡️ Script Prop: ADMIN_PIN (opt.)
  • GEMINI_MODEL (AUTO = latest Flash)
  • START_YEAR_MONTH / MONTHS_AHEAD
  • DEFAULT_START_TIME / END_TIME
  • WHATSAPP_INVITE_URL
  • AUTO_RELEASE_ON_MEMBER_DELETE
