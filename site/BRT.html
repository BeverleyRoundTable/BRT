Established 1950

We are a movement of young, ordinary guys doing extraordinary things. Whether it's trying a new sport, going on an adventure, or raising money for our communities, we aim to have fun, make friends, and live life to the fullest.

Find Friends

Positive energy and camaraderie. Connect with a diverse group of guys and build friendships that last a lifetime. No funny handshakes, just mates doing cool stuff together.

Shake Up Routine

Adventure awaits. From axe throwing to white-water rafting, comedy clubs to brewery tours. Break free from the ordinary and ignite your social life.

Community

Giving back feels good. We're proud to organise charitable events, including our much-loved Christmas Santa Sleigh tour around Beverley, making a tangible impact.
