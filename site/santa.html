Our Build Partners

Meet the team behind the magic! These incredible local organisations provided the equipment, expertise, and storage to bring our electric Tuk-Tuk sleigh to life.

Greens Signmakers

A massive thank you to Greens for transforming our electric tuk-tuk into a show-stopping Santa Sleigh! Operating from their Hull workshop since 1963, this award-winning team provides eco-friendly, end-to-end signage for major names.
